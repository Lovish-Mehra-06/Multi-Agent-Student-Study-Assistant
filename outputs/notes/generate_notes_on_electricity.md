# Study Notes – Electricity (NCERT)

## 1.  Introduction  
- **Electricity** is a controllable and convenient form of energy used in homes, schools, hospitals, industries, etc.  
- It is produced by a **battery or cell** that generates a **potential difference** (voltage) between its two terminals.  
- The potential difference drives electrons to flow, creating an **electric current** through a conductor.

---

## 2.  Electric Current & Circuit  

| Term | Definition (as per the text) |
|------|------------------------------|
| **Electric current** | Flow of electrons through a conductor. |
| **Potential difference** | The energy per unit charge that drives the current. |
| **Battery / Cell** | A source of electrical energy; the chemical reaction inside creates the potential difference. |
