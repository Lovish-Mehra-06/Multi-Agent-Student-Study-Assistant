## Introduction to Electricity
Electricity is a controllable and convenient form of energy for various uses in homes, schools, hospitals, industries, and more. It is essential to understand what constitutes electricity, how it flows in an electric circuit, and the factors that control or regulate the current through an electric circuit.

## Electric Current and Circuit
We are familiar with air current and water current. Similarly, electric current is the flow of electrons in a conductor. An electric circuit is a path through which electric current flows.

## Potential Difference
A device that helps to maintain a potential difference across a conductor is a battery or a cell. The potential difference between two points is said to be 1 V if 1 J of work is done in moving 1 C of charge from one point to the other.

## Energy Given to Each Coulomb of Charge
The energy given to each coulomb of charge passing through a 6 V battery is 6 J.

## Ohm's Law
Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it, provided the temperature remains constant. The formula for Ohm's Law is:
$$V = IR$$
where $V$ is the potential difference, $I$ is the current, and $R$ is the resistance.

## Heating Effect of Electric Current
The chemical reaction within a cell generates the potential difference between its two terminals, setting the electrons in motion to flow the current through a resistor or a system of resistors connected to the battery. The heating effect of electric current is used in various applications.

## Important Facts
* Metals are good conductors of electricity.
* Electric current-carrying wire behaves like a magnet.
* The combination of series and parallel resistors is helpful when each gadget has different resistance and requires different current to operate properly.
