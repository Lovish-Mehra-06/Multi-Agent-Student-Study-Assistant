## 1. What is electric power?

**Q:** Define electric power.  
**A:**

- Electric power is the rate at which electrical energy is delivered or consumed.
- Formula:  
  \[
  P = V I
  \]  
  where \(P\) is power (in watts, W), \(V\) is voltage (in volts, V), and \(I\) is current (in amperes, A).

---

## 2. How is power calculated for a bulb?

**Q:** A bulb is connected to a 220 V generator and draws 0.50 A. What is its power?  
**A:**  
\[
P = V I = 220\,\text{V} \times 0.50\,\text{A} = 110\,\text{W}
\]

---

## 3. How to compute energy consumption?

**Q:** A 400 W refrigerator runs 8 h/day. What is the energy used in 30 days?  
**A:**

- Energy \(E = P \times t\).
- \(E = 400\,\text{W} \times 8\,\text{h/day} \times 30\,\text{days} = 96{,}000\,\text{Wh} = 96\,\text{kWh}\).

---

## 4. What is the cost of electricity?

**Q:** If electricity costs Rs 3.00 per kWh, how much does the refrigerator cost to run for 30 days?  
**A:**  
\[
\text{Cost} = 96\,\text{kWh} \times 3.00\,\text{Rs/kWh} = 288\,\text{Rs}
\]

---

## 5. How to find current from resistance?

**Q:** A bulb filament has resistance \(R = 1200\,\Omega\) and is connected to 220 V. What current does it draw?  
**A:**

- Use Ohm’s law: \(I = V/R\).
- \(I = 220\,\text{V} / 1200\,\Omega \approx 0.183\,\text{A}\).

---

## 6. How to find current for a heater coil?

**Q:** A heater coil has resistance \(R = 100\,\Omega\) and is connected to 220 V. What current does it draw?  
**A:**  
\[
I = \frac{220\,\text{V}}{100\,\Omega} = 2.2\,\text{A}
\]

---

## 7. Current in series and parallel circuits

**Q:** Two 24 Ω coils (A & B) are connected to a 220 V supply.

- (i) Each coil separately
- (ii) Both in series
- (iii) Both in parallel  
  What are the currents?

**A:**  
| Connection | Equivalent Resistance | Current \(I = V/R\) |
|------------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| A alone | 24 Ω | \(220/24 \approx 9.17\) A |
| B alone | 24 Ω | \(220/24 \approx 9.17\) A |
| Series (48 Ω) | 48 Ω | \(220/48 \approx 4.58\) A |
| Parallel (12 Ω) | 12 Ω | \(220/12 \approx 18.33\) A |
