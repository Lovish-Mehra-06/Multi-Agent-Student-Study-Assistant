## Introduction to Light Chapter
The Light chapter covers various topics related to mirrors and their properties.

## Key Concepts
* **Reflection**: The change in direction of light at a surface that causes it to bounce back.
* **Mirror**: A surface that reflects light.
* **Concave Mirror**: A mirror that curves inward, used to converge light rays.
* **Focal Length**: The distance between the mirror and the point where light rays converge.

## Important Definitions
* **Principal Focus (F)**: The point where light rays converge after reflecting off a concave mirror.
* **Centre of Curvature (C)**: The point that is equidistant from all points on the mirror's surface.
* **Pole (P)**: The point on the mirror where the axis of symmetry intersects the surface.

## Formulas
* The focal length of a concave mirror is given by: $f = \frac{R}{2}$, where $R$ is the radius of curvature.
* The relationship between object distance, image distance, and focal length is given by: $\frac{1}{u} + \frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f}$, where $u$ is the object distance, $v$ is the image distance, and $f$ is the focal length.

* **Activity 9.2**: Hold a concave mirror towards the Sun and direct the reflected light onto a sheet of paper to observe the convergence of light rays.

## Important Facts
* A concave mirror can produce a real, inverted image of an object.
* The focal length of a concave mirror is half the radius of curvature.
* Light rays converge at the principal focus of a concave mirror.
