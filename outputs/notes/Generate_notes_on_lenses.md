## 1.  Ray Diagrams for Lenses  

| Lens Type | Key Ray(s) | Behaviour after Refraction |
|-----------|------------|----------------------------|
| **Convex (converging)** | 1. Ray parallel to the principal axis | Passes through the focal point on the *other* side of the lens (Fig. 9.13 (a)). |
| | 2. Ray through the centre of the lens | Continues straight (undeviated). |
| **Concave (diverging)** | 1. Ray parallel to the principal axis | Appears to diverge from the focal point on the *same* side of the lens (Fig. 9.13 (b)). |
| | 2. Ray through the centre of the lens | Continues straight (undeviated). |

> **Note** – The second ray (through the centre) is not shown in the provided excerpts but is a standard part of ray‑diagram construction.

## 2.  Experimental Study – Activity 9.12  

1. **Determine the focal length** of a convex lens (method described in Activity 9.11).  
2. **Mark the focal points** on a table:  
   * Draw five parallel lines spaced by the focal length.  
   * Place the lens on the central line.  
   * Label the lines on either side as \(2F_1, F_1, F_2, 2F_2\).  
3. **Place a candle** (object) far beyond \(2F_1\) on the left side.  
4. **Record** the image on a screen on the opposite side: note its **nature**, **position**, and **relative size**.  
5. **Repeat** the experiment for the following object positions:  
   * Just behind \(2F_1\)  
   * Between \(F_1\) and \(2F_1\)  
   * At \(F_1\)  
   * Between \(F_1\) and the object (O)

## 3.  Summary of Image Properties (Convex Lens)  

| Object Position | Image Nature | Image Position | Relative Size |
|-----------------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| Far beyond \(2F\) | Real, inverted, smaller | On the opposite side, between \(F\) and \(2F\) | < 1 |
| Just behind \(2F\) | Real, inverted, smaller | Between \(F\) and \(2F\) | < 1 |
| Between \(F\) and \(2F\) | Real, inverted, larger | Beyond \(2F\) | > 1 |
